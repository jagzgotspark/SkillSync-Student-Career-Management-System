❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the mentorship program free?
+
The first intro session (30 minutes) with any mentor is completely free. Ongoing sessions are available on a subscription or pay-per-session basis at very affordable student prices.
How are mentors verified?
+
Every mentor goes through a verification process including LinkedIn profile review, company email verification, and a screening interview with our team. We only onboard professionals with at least 3 years of relevant experience.
Can I switch mentors if it's not a good fit?
+
Absolutely! If you feel your current mentor isn't the right match, you can request a switch at any time with no questions asked. We want to ensure you get the best possible guidance.
How often are sessions held?
+
Session frequency is agreed upon between you and your mentor. Most mentors offer weekly or bi-weekly sessions of 45-60 minutes. You can also schedule ad-hoc sessions for specific needs like pre-interview prep.
Can I become a mentor on SkillSync?
+
Yes! If you're an industry professional or an alumni with at least 3 years of experience, we'd love to have you. Click "Become a Mentor" to apply. Mentors on SkillSync earn recognition and can optionally receive session fees.
